## Option 1 — PhD in Computer Science ★ Best UCT option

**Department:** Computer Science, School of Information Technology (SIT)  
**Faculty:** Science  
**Degree awarded:** `PhD in Computer Science`  
**URL:** https://sit.uct.ac.za/phd-computer-science

### What the degree involves

Research degree examined by thesis alone. The thesis must constitute a substantial contribution to knowledge, show evidence of original investigation, and give a full statement of the literature. The candidate conducts independent research under supervision appointed by Senate.

### Admission process

1. Identify a willing supervisor by contacting academic staff directly in your area of interest
2. Once a supervisor agrees, apply through UCT's central applications system
3. Application must include a research proposal naming the supervisor
4. **No opening or closing dates** — rolling applications year-round; outcome notified soon after completing the process

This is explicitly supervisor-gated — no application proceeds without a named supervisor.

### Admission requirements — coursework MSc eligibility

The PhD in CS page does **not** require a full research thesis MSc. UCT's PhD admission is supervisor-mediated: if a supervisor accepts you, formal admission follows. A coursework MSc with mini-thesis is the standard South African Honours+MSc pathway and is widely accepted at UCT for PhD entry. **This is less restrictive than Stellenbosch E&E's explicit thesis-based MSc requirement** — a meaningful advantage given Johannes's qualification.

## Option 3 — PhD in Information Systems

**Department:** Information Systems, School of Information Technology (SIT)  
**Faculty:** Commerce  
**Degree awarded:** `PhD in Information Systems`

Relevant only if the research is framed primarily as DSR/IS rather than NLP/engineering. The IS department handles ICT4D, HCI, and information systems design research. Less suited to the NLP and ASR technical contributions but potentially appropriate if the DSR methodology and AT design framing dominate.

## Admission Requirements — Coursework MSc

UCT's PhD admission is supervisor-mediated and does not publish a blanket thesis-vs-coursework restriction for the CS PhD. A coursework MSc with mini-thesis is widely accepted. **This makes UCT materially less risky than Stellenbosch E&E for Johannes's specific qualification**, where the E&E department explicitly requires a thesis-based MSc.
