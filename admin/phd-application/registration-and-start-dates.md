### PhD registration 2026

From the SU registration schedule page (`su.ac.za/en/students/registration-and-fees/registration-schedule`):

- **12 January 2026** — Online registration opens for postgraduate students
- **26 January – 30 January 2026** — On-campus assisted registration (senior/PG students)
- **20 February 2026** — Final day for late registration for continuing M and D students
- **26 March 2026** — Final registration day for **newcomer M and D students** (new PhD entrants)

The 26 March deadline is the effective start-of-year cutoff for new doctoral students. A new PhD student who completes the application process and secures supervisor agreement before this date can register in the same academic year.

### Mid-year (July) start

The second semester begins 20 July. The registration schedule does not list an explicit July registration window for new doctoral candidates — the 26 March date is the only published cutoff for newcomer M and D students. Whether a July start is possible for a new PhD should be confirmed with the relevant faculty office. For the E&E department, contact Ms Nicole Hartzenburg (`nicolepa@sun.ac.za`).

### 2027 intake applications

- Applications for the 2027 intake opened **1 April 2026** — currently open
- Faculty-specific closing dates apply (not published universally on the overview page)
- E&E Engineering PhD deadline for 2026 intake was 31 January 2026, suggesting the 2027 deadline will be approximately **31 January 2027**
- Science Faculty (CS) is supervisor-gated and rolling — contact the department directly
- **Supervisor agreement must be in place before submitting any application**

### Thesis submission deadlines (for planning purposes)

For **Faculty of Engineering** (E&E):
- PhD thesis for December letter of completion: 1 September
- PhD thesis for March/April graduation: 11 August

For **Faculty of Science** (CS):
- PhD thesis for December letter of completion: 2 October
- PhD thesis for March/April graduation: 18 August

### PhD registration 2026

From the UCT registration page (`uct.ac.za/students/registration`):

- **21 January – 7 February 2026** — Assisted (in-person) registration

Faculty-specific registration dates for doctoral candidates are not centrally published — confirm with the relevant faculty office.

### PhD admissions model (CS/SIT)

From the UCT CS PhD page (`sit.uct.ac.za/phd-computer-science`), verbatim:

> **"There are no opening or closing dates for applications. You can apply at any stage. You will be notified of the outcome of your application soon after you have completed the application process."**

This means a PhD in Computer Science at UCT can be started at **any point in the year**, subject to supervisor availability and administrative registration. A July start is plausible under this model.

### 2027 intake applications

- General postgraduate applications for 2027 opened **1 April 2026** — currently open
- General PG closing date: **30 September 2026** (firm — "we do not consider late application requests")
- CS PhD: **rolling, no fixed deadline** — the 30 September date applies to structured programmes, not research PhDs per the SIT page
- **Supervisor agreement must be secured before applying**

## What This Means in Practice

### Earliest possible start date

**January 2027** is the most realistic earliest start for a new PhD at either institution, assuming:
- Supervisor conversations happen April–June 2026
- Supervisor agreement confirmed by July–August 2026
- Application submitted August–September 2026
- Admission confirmed October–November 2026
- Registration: January 2027 (SU) or January–February 2027 (UCT)

**Could a late 2026 start happen?** In theory, the SU March 2026 deadline has passed for 2026. UCT's rolling CS admissions leave a theoretical window — if a UCT supervisor agreed and the application was processed quickly, a July 2026 semester 2 start is not impossible. But practically, given that supervisor conversations have not yet begun as of April 2026, this is unlikely. **Plan for January 2027.**

### Critical path to January 2027 start

```
April 2026    — Conversations with Schalk and Jaco (internal buy-in)
May 2026      — Jaco introduces to Kamper/Niesler (SU) or Densmore/Keet (UCT)
June 2026     — Supervisor conversation; research proposal refined
July 2026     — Supervisor agreement confirmed in writing
Aug–Sep 2026  — Formal application submitted (SU: before Jan 2027 deadline; UCT: before 30 Sep 2026 for structured, rolling for CS PhD)
Oct–Nov 2026  — Admission decision
Jan 2027      — Register and start
```

For SU E&E, the formal application deadline is approximately 31 January 2027 — so the above timeline has slack. For UCT general PG, the 30 September 2026 deadline is tighter but still achievable.
