## Option 1 — PhD (Electrical Engineering) ★ Recommended

**Department:** Electrical & Electronic Engineering  
**Faculty:** Engineering  
**Degree awarded:** `Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Electrical Engineering`  
**Division:** Signal Processing & Machine Learning

### Why this fits

The Signal Processing & Machine Learning division explicitly lists speech recognition, biometrics, and telecommunications as its domain. Its active projects include:

- *Afrilator* — English-Afrikaans Speech Translator (Prof Niesler's group)
- English-isiXhosa speech translation
- Speech synthesis, text-to-speech, automatic speech translation

This is not a tangential fit — the group is already working on Afrikaans speech technology. The adult Afrikaans clinical vocabulary corpus and the ASR pipeline (Feature B) land directly in this division's existing research stream.

**Prof Kamper** is the strongest primary supervisor candidate. His stated research focus is "methods that allow machines to acquire language autonomously, using as little supervision as possible" — a direct description of the low-resource Afrikaans NLP and personalisation-under-sparse-data contributions. He runs an active research group with students.

**Prof Niesler** has directly complementary expertise: the Afrilator project is the closest existing work in the world to Feature B (Afrikaans ASR + generation). A co-supervision arrangement between Kamper (low-resource NLP/ML) and Niesler (Afrikaans speech) would cover the full technical breadth of the PhD.

## Option 2 — PhD in Computer Science

**Department:** Mathematical Sciences (Computer Science Division)  
**Faculty:** Science  
**Degree awarded:** `PhD in Computer Science`

### Why this fits

The CS Division lists Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning explicitly in its research areas. More specifically, it has:

- An active assistive technologies research thread (Prof van Zijl)
- Multilingual NLP and language generation expertise (Prof Pretorius)
- HCI and social impact computing (Prof Tucker)

The DSR methodology and the assistive technology artefact framing sit more naturally in CS than in E&E. If the PhD is positioned primarily as an AT/HCI design research project with NLP contributions, CS is the better home.

### Key supervisors

| Name | Research area | Contact |

| **Prof Laurette Pretorius** *(extraordinary)* | **Multilingual NLP**, semantic computing, ontology, machine translation, language generation | — |
| Prof Steve Kroon | AI/ML, statistical learning theory | `kroon@sun.ac.za` |

**Prof van Zijl** is the most precise fit within CS: she works on assistive technologies, which is rare within a CS department and unusually well-matched to an AAC artefact project. A CS PhD supervised by van Zijl with E&E co-supervision from Kamper/Niesler would combine AT credibility with technical depth.

**Prof Pretorius** (extraordinary professor) works in multilingual NLP and language generation — directly relevant to the Afrikaans vocabulary corpus and constrained generation components.

### Admission requirements

- Thesis-based MSc in Computer Science required
- "Prospective students must identify and contact a potential supervisor in their field before applying. Applications without prior supervisor engagement cannot be considered." — this is a hard requirement, not a recommendation
- Highly competitive; capacity-limited

### Application process

- Contact `postgrad@cs.sun.ac.za` first
- Faculty Administrator: Ms Shivvon Ruiters — `shivvon@sun.ac.za`
- No hard public deadline — the process is supervisor-gated; applications are considered once a supervisor has agreed
- Application portal: `student.sun.ac.za`

- "Electrical Engineering" from Stellenbosch in the context of speech/NLP is a known and respected pedigree — Kamper and Niesler are internationally published; the degree is associated with their research group

## Recommended Application Pathway

### Step 1 — Identify the right supervisor via Jaco (immediate)

Before contacting SU directly, use Jaco du Toit's existing Stellenbosch network to get a warm introduction to Prof Kamper or Prof Niesler. Both departments explicitly require prior supervisor contact — a cold email to Kamper works, but an introduction from Jaco who already co-supervises in the SA academic system is materially stronger.

**Target introduction:** Prof Herman Kamper (`kamperh@sun.ac.za`) as primary supervisor + Prof Thomas Niesler as co-supervisor for the Afrikaans ASR component.

### Step 2 — Initial supervisor contact (within 1–2 months)

Once Jaco has made introductions (or after a warm cold email), contact Prof Kamper with:
- A one-page research summary (the four-way gap and three-contribution structure)
- The PhD framing review document (`literature/reviews/phd-ai-framing-review.md`) as supporting material
- The deployed artefact as evidence of seriousness — the PWA is live and in daily use; this is rare at the application stage

Do not lead with the Karen Smit or Schalk Heunis angles in the academic approach — lead with the research problem and the existing work.

### Step 3 — Formal application (2027 intake)

With supervisor agreement confirmed:
- Submit via `student.sun.ac.za`
- Deadline: approximately **31 January 2027**
- Include Jaco's Vodacom co-supervision arrangement and Schalk's endorsement letter as part of the industry partnership documentation

### Step 4 — Resolve MSc eligibility ⚠️ Risk item

Johannes holds an MSc in Data Science & AI completed via **coursework + mini-thesis** (not a full research thesis). The E&E department explicitly requires a "thesis-based master's in a relevant engineering discipline." A coursework-based MSc with a mini-thesis is generally not considered equivalent to a full research MSc in South African postgraduate admission criteria.

**Three possible outcomes:**

| Outcome | Implication |
|---|---|
| SU E&E accepts the mini-thesis MSc as equivalent (supervisor discretion) | No obstacle — proceed with E&E application |
| SU E&E requires a full research MSc | Either do a full research MSc first (+1–2 years) or switch to CS route |
| CS Division accepts the MSc as sufficient | CS becomes the primary route regardless of E&E ruling |

**What to do:** Raise this directly with Prof Kamper in the first supervisor conversation — before any formal application. Supervisors often have discretion on admission equivalencies and can advocate for a candidate they want. Do not raise it with the administrator first; raise it with the potential supervisor, who can then confirm with the faculty if needed.

**Fallback:** If E&E is blocked, the CS route (Prof van Zijl + Prof Pretorius) is a clean alternative. The CS Division's admission wording is less prescriptive about thesis vs. coursework, and an MSc in Data Science & AI maps naturally to a CS PhD. The degree title (PhD in Computer Science) is also a reasonable credential for the intended career path.

**Do not assume this is a disqualifier** — it is a risk to confirm, not a definitive block. Many South African universities assess coursework MSc + mini-thesis candidates on a case-by-case basis, particularly when the candidate has strong industry experience and a well-developed research proposal.
