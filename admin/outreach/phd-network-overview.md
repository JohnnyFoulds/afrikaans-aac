## What "Vodacom-Facilitated PhD" Means

This phrase appears throughout this document and in the PhD framing review. It needs a precise definition because it is doing real work — and it covers a wide range of possible arrangements.

### The contrast: self-directed vs. facilitated

**Self-funded / self-directed PhD:** You register at a university, pay fees yourself, do research in your evenings and weekends. Vodacom has no formal role. Your employment there is background context, not structure — you happen to work there, but the PhD has no institutional relationship with Vodacom.

**Vodacom-facilitated PhD:** Vodacom provides something tangible — time, data, co-supervision, or formal partnership — and has an institutional relationship with the research, not just a personal one. Exactly what that means depends on what Schalk agrees to and what Jaco's co-supervision arrangement looks like.

### The spectrum of facilitation

Facilitation is not binary. It sits on a spectrum from light to heavy:

| Level | What it looks like | What it requires |
|---|---|---|
| **Light** | 20% time allocation informally agreed with Schalk; Jaco's name on a co-supervision form in his personal capacity | Schalk's informal agreement; Jaco's willingness |
| **Medium** | Formal Vodacom research partnership letter to the university; Jaco as named Vodacom co-supervisor; research outputs acknowledged as partly Vodacom-generated | Schalk's sign-off; HR or legal awareness; university partnership agreement |
| **Heavy** | Vodacom named as industry partner in the university registration; data-sharing agreement for Vodacom data or infrastructure; partial fee sponsorship or bursary; formal IP agreement governing research outputs | Executive approval; legal; finance |

The minimum viable version — light facilitation — is probably achievable through Schalk alone, without any formal process. The medium and heavy versions require institutional steps on both the Vodacom and university sides.

### Why the level of facilitation matters

**For the university application:** Universities actively prefer industry co-sponsors. A Stellenbosch application that arrives with a named Vodacom co-supervisor and a partnership letter is materially stronger than a cold academic application. The facilitation level directly affects how the application is received.

**For data and infrastructure access:** Some research questions in the PhD — particularly the deployment characterisation and any work touching Vodacom network data — require formal access agreements. Informal access is fragile; it depends on individuals staying in their roles. A formal data-sharing agreement survives personnel changes.

**For the Vodacom leverage goal:** If Vodacom's role is informal, the "Vodacom leverage" argument in the PhD framing is aspirational. If Vodacom is a named research partner with formal outputs, that leverage is concrete — the research feeds into Vodacom's African language AI capability in a documented, durable way.

**For IP:** The heavier the facilitation, the more important it is to clarify IP ownership upfront. Vodacom has standard IP terms for employee-generated work. If the PhD produces a corpus, a trained model, or design principles, who owns them? This needs to be agreed before Schalk's informal support becomes a formal partnership, not after.

### The practical goal

The conversation with Schalk is aimed at landing somewhere in the light-to-medium range: informal time allocation plus Jaco's co-supervision. That is enough to strengthen the university application, provide legitimate research time, and establish the Vodacom connection without requiring executive sign-off or a legal process. The heavier levels are worth pursuing if the research produces outputs Vodacom actively wants to own or commercialise — but that conversation comes later, once the PhD is underway and the outputs are clearer.

### Schalk Heunis — Internal Sponsor

Schalk is the gatekeeper for everything Vodacom provides: research time, data access, internal endorsement. Without his buy-in, the "Vodacom-facilitated PhD" framing collapses. He holds a PhD himself and has been a practising data scientist since 2002, so he understands both the academic trajectory and the business case simultaneously.

**His role is not supervision — it is sponsorship.** He needs to believe the PhD produces something Vodacom can use: an adult Afrikaans NLP corpus, a replicable methodology for African language AI, an edge deployment characterisation applicable to Vodacom's other low-connectivity product contexts.

**Key dynamic:** He is Johannes's direct manager, which means this conversation is also a performance conversation in some sense — framing the PhD as strategic, not personal, matters here more than anywhere else.

## Sequencing: Who to Talk to First

The conversations are not independent — they build on each other. The right sequence is:

```
Step 1 — Schalk (this month)
  Establish internal buy-in and sponsorship framing before any external moves.
  Output: Schalk's informal endorsement and clarity on what Vodacom can offer.

Step 2 — Jaco (this month, in parallel with or just after Schalk)
  Technical conversation, not a formal ask. "Get your take on this."
  Output: Jaco's read on the research framing + introduction to potential Stellenbosch supervisors.

Step 3 — Stellenbosch supervisor (via Jaco, within 1–2 months)
  Formal approach with full PhD framing document and Vodacom backing established.
  Output: Supervisor interest and institutional pathway clarified.

Step 4 — Karen (April 2026 — before DEIB Summit, 14–15 May)
  Wife contacts first; Johannes follows up after positive response.
  Not contingent on Steps 1–3 — can run in parallel, but the human story must come first.
  Output: Karen's engagement and identification of how she wants to champion the project.
```

**Why this order matters:**

- Schalk first because without his endorsement, the "Vodacom-facilitated PhD" framing has no substance. Going to a university before Schalk knows about it risks the relationship.
- Jaco second (or simultaneous) because his institutional connections are needed to identify the right supervisor, and his endorsement strengthens the Stellenbosch application.
- Supervisor third because the application is strongest when it arrives with Vodacom backing already established, not as a cold academic pitch.
- Karen in parallel because her thread is independent — it runs through the wife's personal relationship and does not depend on the institutional pathway being decided.

## Longer-Term Network Effects

If all four roles are filled, the PhD sits at the intersection of:

- **Vodacom AI strategy** (Schalk) — internal credibility and resource access
- **Academic rigour** (Jaco + Stellenbosch supervisor) — publishable research, institutional legitimacy
- **Disability inclusion ecosystem** (Karen) — external visibility, ethics grounding, pan-African reach

This is not a typical PhD configuration. Most applied AI PhDs are either purely academic or purely industry-facing. This one has a credible claim to all three simultaneously — which is also why the framing needs to be maintained carefully: each audience hears the right version of the same project, without any version being false.
