## Design rules applied

- **Max 2 taps** to any phrase (Home → Category → Phrase)
- **9 buttons per category screen** (3×3 grid, ≥25mm targets)
- **Always-visible on every screen:** Noodgeval (emergency) + Roep vir Mom
- **Text-only** (intact reading comprehension)
- **Audio:** one pre-generated MP3 per phrase (edge-tts, af-ZA-WillemNeural)
