**Purpose:** Capture all usable research data from the live v1 deployment before any of it is lost. This data feeds directly into the PhD thesis (Chapter 4) and the first publication.

## Action 1 — Mom baseline interview (this week)

**Format:** Voice recorded conversation, 20–30 minutes. WhatsApp voice note or phone call is fine.

**Why now:** This captures her memory of communication *before* the app. Once she adapts to the new routine, the baseline is gone.

**Questions to ask:**

Pre-app communication:
- What did Dad most struggle to communicate before the app?
- What did a typical "stuck" moment look like — walk me through one?
- How did you resolve it when he couldn't get his message across?
- How frustrated did he seem (1–10) during those moments?
- How frustrated were you (1–10)?
- What did you worry about most regarding his communication?

First impressions of the app:
- What was his reaction when he first used it?
- What was yours?
- Was anything confusing or hard to explain?

**What to do with the recording:**
- Save it (WhatsApp, Voice Memos, anywhere)
- Note the date
- You do not need to transcribe it immediately — the recording is the data

## Action 2 — Usage log (ongoing from today)

Ask Mom to send you a WhatsApp message after each session Dad uses the app. It does not need to be formal — a voice note is fine. Capture:

- Date
- Roughly how long he used it
- Any phrases he kept coming back to
- Any moment he got frustrated and why
- Any phrase he clearly wanted but couldn't find (write it down)

**Frequency:** After each use, or daily summary if he uses it multiple times.

**Storage:** Forward all messages to a dedicated WhatsApp chat or copy them into a running notes file here:
`topics/android/subtopics/aac/notes/usage-log.md`

You (Johannes) create the file and update it as messages come in.

## Action 4 — Consent record (before any data is used)

For a family member in a single-participant case study, formal ethics approval is not required at this stage. A written consent record is required.

**What it needs to say (one paragraph):**
- Dad was aware the app was being evaluated for research purposes
- Mom agreed to be interviewed and for her observations to be used
- Data will be anonymised in any publication (Dad = "Participant 1", Mom = "primary caregiver")
- Either party can withdraw at any time

**Format:** A note signed by you and Mom (WhatsApp message confirming she understands is acceptable for Phase 0). Before formal ethics submission (Phase 1), upgrade to a proper consent form.

**Save as:** `topics/android/subtopics/aac/notes/consent-record.md`

## Action 5 — Ongoing outcome measures

Track these consistently from now. Simple enough that Mom will actually do it.

| Measure | How to collect | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Communication initiations via app | Mom estimates daily count | Daily (loose) |
| Missing phrases | Mom notes any phrase he wanted but couldn't find | Per session |
| Frustration events | Mom notes when he abandons the app mid-attempt | Per session |
| Caregiver confidence (1–10) | Mom self-rates in a WhatsApp message to Johannes | Weekly |
| App reliability issues | Johannes notes any crashes, audio failures, deploy issues | As they occur |

**Storage:** All captured in `usage-log.md` (Action 2). No separate spreadsheet needed at this stage.
